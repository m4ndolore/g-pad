# Project Status

- **Lifecycle:** Active product
- **Brain role:** I/O surface (not the brain)
- **Enclave:** `personal`

g-pad is an e-ink writing pad that works right out of the box and is designed
to make the most of gstack and gbrain. It captures ink, shows a reply, and
syncs when connected. It does not own the corpus or the synthesis engine.
It must work with no brain configured (capture still lands locally) and
plugs into gbrain / gstack when those are present.

Sister I/O: anymouse (desktop capture). Mutual benefactor: Vellum (collaborative
editing surface; retrieval/answer engine is shared, not rebuilt here).
